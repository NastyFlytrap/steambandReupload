Original text on the main page:

Steamband is a Steampunk / Victorian / Pulp variant of Angband, A roguelike ASCII dungeon exploration simulation game. Steamband was based off the 2.9.3 version of Angband source code. You can find out more about Angband by visiting [rephial](https://web.archive.org/web/20221220190249/http://rephial.org/), the Angband homepage.

Be sure to take a look at some of the entertaining writing by many players of Steamband over in the [Verbose Dumps](https://web.archive.org/web/20221220190249/http://angband.oook.cz/steamband/verbosedump.html) section. Many tales of victory and woe await you there.

There are several other resources listed below. Make sure and after you play Steamband to post your Dump on the oook ladder, so you can see how you compare to other players. :-) You can reach oook at [http://angband.oook.cz](https://web.archive.org/web/20221220190249/http://angband.oook.cz/)

There is also a Steamband mailing list. Files, and polls and discussion about the game are available there. You can join by sending an email to [steamband-subscribe@yahoogroups.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20221220190249/mailto:steamband-subscribe@yahoogroups.com) You can also visit the Yahoo groups page [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20221220190249/http://groups.yahoo.com/group/Steamband/).

There is extensive online help in Steamband 0.4.1. Hitting '?' while a skill, race, or class is selected will give you information on it. Hitting enter will display a menu showing all the commands. Hitting '?' will display help, and you can access a variety of FAQ's that provide basic information on how Steam is different than other variants.
